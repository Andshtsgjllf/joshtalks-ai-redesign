Our Story

Why we exist.

Josh Talks started as India's largest vernacular storytelling platform — reaching millions of people across districts, languages, and socioeconomic contexts who rarely saw themselves represented in mainstream media.

That reach revealed something fundamental: the voice AI systems being built for India were trained on data that didn't sound like India. Clean studio recordings, scripted sentences, urban accents. Real speech — spontaneous, emotional, dialect-rich, code-switched — was missing from training data that would power the next generation of voice technology.

So we built the infrastructure to collect it, curate it, and make it available to the researchers and labs who could use it. That work became Josh Talks AI.

"Voice AI has learned to recognise speech and generate speech. What it has not yet learned well is how to participate in conversation."
— Shobhit Banga, Co-Founder, Josh Talks

Why It Matters

Models improve when
measured well.

Voice AI evaluation is harder than it looks. Automated metrics miss what actually matters to listeners. Without rigorous human measurement, model builders can't tell what's improving and what isn't.

📊

Better Data

Models are bounded by their training data. Data that fails to represent the accents, dialects, and speaking contexts of real users produces models that fail real users — regardless of benchmark scores on clean speech.

Finding from Voice of India Major global models show significant performance gaps on Indian accents and regional dialects — even those claiming multilingual support.
🔬

Better Evaluation

Automated metrics like WER penalise natural spelling variation and miss what listeners actually notice. Human pairwise evaluation surfaces failures in naturalness, expressiveness, and contextual appropriateness that no automated metric can detect at scale.

VoiceArena approach Blind pairwise votes from real listeners across 9 TTS models and 6 languages. No brand bias. No lab access. Public results.
🚀

Better Models

When evaluation is accurate and data is representative, model builders can identify exactly what to fix — and verify that fixes actually work. The feedback loop between measurement and improvement is what drives progress in voice AI.

The goal Voice AI systems that understand how people in India actually speak — not how studio recordings assume they speak.
Research Philosophy

The principles
behind the work.

01

Models improve when measured.

Evaluation is not a final check — it is how progress happens. Without independent, rigorous measurement, model builders cannot distinguish real improvement from benchmark overfitting.

02

Data improves when audited.

Quality is not a one-time property of a dataset. It requires continuous verification — human review, anomaly detection, and full traceability from source to label.

03

Evaluation improves when humans stay in the loop.

Automated metrics are fast and scalable. Human judgment is what they're approximating. For voice AI — where what matters is how speech sounds to a listener — human evaluation is irreplaceable.

04

Diversity is not a footnote.

India has 22 constitutionally recognised languages and hundreds of dialects. Building voice AI that works for India means building for that diversity from day one — not retrofitting it later.
